## Memberships

Memberships are monthly. The plans, prices and what each one includes (reformer, sauna and red light sessions) are those shown on the pricing page and in the app at the time you sign up.

## First session

The first session is free, lasts 45 minutes and is required before you train on your own. It explains how the studio works and how to use the reformer safely. It is not a medical assessment.

## Bookings

Each booking is a 1-hour slot: up to 50 minutes of class plus time to come in, get set up and leave. You book in advance in the app, and cancellation is free up to 2 hours before.

## Cancelling your membership

You can cancel your membership with 10 days' notice.

## Founding members

The founding price stays the same for as long as you remain a member. If you cancel and come back, you join at the current rate.

## Minimum age

18 to train on your own. Minors from 16, accompanied by an adult.

## In the studio

When you finish, leave your reformer ready for the next person.
