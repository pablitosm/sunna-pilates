A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for the website to work, and others are only used if you accept them.

## Technical cookies (always on)

They don't need your consent because they make the website work the way you ask it to.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sunna-idioma | SUNNA | Remember the language you choose in the selector | 1 year |
| sunna-consent (local storage) | SUNNA | Remember your choice about cookies | Until you delete it |
| sunna_sesion | SUNNA | Keep you signed in while the website is in private access | 30 days |
| sunna_panel | SUNNA | Admin panel session (SUNNA team only) | 12 hours |

## Analytics and marketing cookies (only if you accept them)

They only load if they are enabled on the website and you accept them in the cookie notice. If you don't accept them, none are stored.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _ga, _ga_* | Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) | Measure how the website is used | 2 years |
| _fbp | Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) | Measure how well our ads work | 3 months |

Google and Meta may process this data outside the European Economic Area. We explain this in the Privacy policy.

## Our own analytics, without cookies

We also measure, in aggregate, how the website is used with our own tool, which uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. We explain it in the Privacy policy.

## Maps and links

The map on the website is a drawing and loads nothing from third parties. If you tap «Open in maps», Google Maps opens in a separate tab, with its own terms and cookies. The same goes for Instagram, WhatsApp and the app stores.

## How to change your choice

You can change it whenever you like from «Cookie preferences», at the bottom of the website. If you withdraw your consent, we delete any Google Analytics or Meta cookies already in your browser. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.
