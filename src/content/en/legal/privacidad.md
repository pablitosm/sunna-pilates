## Data controller

SUNNA Pilates · C/ San Francisco Javier 5, bajo exterior, 38001 Santa Cruz de Tenerife.
For any question about your data: info@sunnapilates.es.

## What data we process and why

- **Waiting list and founding members.** If you sign up on the website, we store your name, email, phone number and language to let you know about the opening and the founding member offer. We also store the date and the IP address you sent it from, as proof of your consent. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We keep this data until the studio opens and for no more than 12 months after that, unless you become a member.
- **Bookings, payments and studio access.** These are handled with bsport (bookings and payments) and Kisi (opening the door with your phone), which process your data on behalf of SUNNA. Legal basis: your membership contract.
- **When you write to us** on WhatsApp, Instagram or by email, we use your data to reply. Legal basis: your request.
- **Website analytics.** See the next section.

## Website analytics

To learn what people find interesting on the website and improve it, we measure in aggregate how it is used: which pages are visited, which buttons and links are tapped, which sections are seen, how long and how far each page is scrolled, where visitors come from (for example, Google or Instagram), the type of device and the language.

- **No cookies:** we store nothing in your browser.
- **No IP address:** we don't store it. To count visitors we use an anonymous identifier calculated from the IP address and the browser that changes every day, so it can't tell who you are or follow you from one day to the next.
- **No third parties:** the data stays on our own server and is not shared with anyone.
- **If your browser asks not to be tracked** (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), we don't measure your visit.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used in order to improve it. You can object by writing to us, or by turning on the do-not-track option in your browser. This data is kept for a maximum of 13 months and then deleted automatically.

## Google Analytics and Meta Pixel

Only if they are enabled on the website and you accept them in the cookie notice. Google and Meta may transfer data to the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can withdraw your consent at any time from «Cookie preferences», at the bottom of the website. More details in the Cookie policy.

## Who else accesses your data

We don't sell or share your data. Only the providers who help us deliver the service (website hosting, bsport and Kisi and, if you accept them, Google and Meta) have access to it, under contract and only for what we ask them to do, or the authorities when the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, object to its processing, and withdraw your consent, by writing to info@sunnapilates.es. If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (Spanish Data Protection Agency, aepd.es).
