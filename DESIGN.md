# Design System: SUNNA

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Warm Sheet"**

SUNNA is a single warm sheet of cream paper (the "lámina") lit by one orange sun. Everything visual comes from the SUNNA Web reference canvas (1440px); the PDF brief supplies the words. The page reads as a calm editorial poster: large regular-weight DM Sans headlines on cream, small uppercase tracked labels at card corners, soft peach cards with 28px corners, and two moments of weight where the sheet turns dark: the Tinta Recovery band and the Naranja 900 founders band. A fine film grain sits over the whole page so the cream never reads as flat screen white.

Depth comes from light rather than structure: radial orange halos (closing sun arc, the glow behind the app drawing), warm-tinted shadows, and fine hairlines between content. No glass or blur on any surface.

Content that the brief has not yet supplied is shown honestly, in a small monospace `[PENDIENTE]` marker, never faked.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cream ground (#fdf6ee) with a 3.5% multiply film grain over everything.
- One orange voice (Naranja 500) for the CTA pill, sun halos, map pin, active arrows and badges.
- Regular weight everywhere; size and tight tracking carry hierarchy, not boldness.
- Uppercase 11px labels tracked +16% name places, groups, card titles and data.
- Two dark bands (Tinta, Naranja 900) and one dark highlighted card (Premium) break the cream rhythm.
- Solid surfaces only: no glass, translucency or backdrop blur.
- Arch portals, circle glyphs and a rising sun arc as the shape vocabulary.

## Colors

A single warm orange ramp over cream, with a deep brown ink doing all dark work.

### Primary
- **Sun Orange / Naranja 500**: the signature. Fills the CTA pill, membership badge, FAQ-card arrow discs, the map pin, focus ring, and the cores of every halo and the closing sun. Text on it is always Tinta.
- **Ember / Naranja 700**: forms, never text blocks. Glyph strokes, the em-dash bullets on membership lists, the founders counter figure, the late-shift hours in the schedule, dashed borders.
- **Burnt Sienna / Naranja 900**: the CTA arrow disc, and the only orange that carries running text (secondary links, address link, WhatsApp icon, FAQ plus sign) and the field of the founders band and footer.

### Secondary
- **Peach ramp / Naranja 50–300**: Naranja 50 is the default card surface; Naranja 100 the question cards and field hairlines; Naranja 200 hairline borders, selection, grid lines on the map and the hero tint; Naranja 300 hover state of the CTA and the accent (numbers, bullets, prices per session) on dark fields.

### Red light (Rojo)
A warm red ramp, a close relative of the orange, reserved for the red-light room (client decision, 2026-09-28). It lives only in the "La sala de luz roja" row of the Recovery section and on the "Luz roja" arch in El estudio.
- **Rojo 500**: the core of the red-light arch glow and the em-dash bullets of that row. Never text.
- **Rojo 200–300**: the bright centre of the arch glow; Rojo 100–200 the Estudio arch.
- **Rojo 50 / 100**: the LED dots and panel outlines drawn over the glow.
- **Rojo 700 / 900**: the edge of the arch glow; Rojo 900 is the only red that may carry text (the corner label of that row, the Estudio glyph).
- **Rojo halo** (Rojo 500 at 18–20%): the soft radial light behind the red-light row, fading to nothing (The Light-Not-Line Rule applies).

### Neutral
- **Crema**: page ground, chips, cards on dark bands, text on dark bands.
- **Tostado**: alternate section ground (How it works), photo placeholders, map base.
- **Tinta**: all body text, the Recovery band, the highlighted Premium card, dark buttons.
- **Tinta 60**: secondary text, labels, pending markers.
- **Línea / Línea fuerte**: 12% and 22% Tinta hairlines for dividers.
- **Blanco / Vidrio luz 55–65**: pure white only as the booking field fill and as the translucent white highlight inside the glass recipe (gradient start, border, cursor halo). Never a page or card ground.

### Named Rules
**The Orange-Is-Not-Ink Rule.** Text is Tinta, Tinta 60, Crema (on dark) or Naranja 900. Naranja 500 and 700 never set text; text sitting on Naranja 500 is Tinta.

**The Two Dark Bands Rule.** Dark surfaces are deliberate and few: the Tinta Recovery band, the Naranja 900 founders band and footer, and the one highlighted membership card. Everything else stays on cream, tostado or peach.

**The Light-Not-Line Rule.** Emphasis and atmosphere come from radial orange light fading to cream, never from saturated flat blocks of Naranja 500 larger than a button or badge.

## Typography

**Display Font:** DM Sans Variable (optical sizing on), fallback ui-sans-serif, system-ui
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace (SFMono-Regular, Menlo) for `[PENDIENTE]` markers only

**Character:** One humanist grotesque at Regular weight, set large and tight for headlines and open (1.6) for text; tabular numerals throughout so prices and times align.

### Hierarchy
The reference system uses these real sizes (px at 1440, fluid clamps shrink them on mobile): 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21/22, 24, 27, 34, 36, 40, 44, 52, 56, 60, 82.

- **Display** (400, 44→82px, 1.02, −0.025em): the hero headline only, max 11ch.
- **Headline** (400, 36→56px, 1.06, −0.02em): every section title. Founders title runs to 60px (1.04); card figures (class lengths) reach 56px.
- **Figure** (400, 40→52px, 1, −0.03em): membership prices; founders counter 40px; schedule hours 28→40px.
- **Question** (400, 26→34px, 1.15, −0.02em): "¿Y si…?" question cards. Mobile menu links 36px.
- **Title** (400, 21→27px, 1.15, −0.015em): step titles; fixed 24px card and idea titles; FAQ summaries 18→21px; the FAQ plus sign 22px.
- **Lead / large body** (400, 18–19px, 1.5–1.6): hero subtitle 18px, CTA label 19px, Recovery line 19px, closing text 19px, large chips 19px.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.6): default text; 16px in cards, lists and the footer.
- **Small** (400, 13–15px): nav links and compact CTA 15px, small text 14px, chips, footer base line and Recovery notice 13px, field and badge labels 12px.
- **Label** (400, 11px, +0.16em, UPPERCASE, Tinta 60): the `.etiqueta` corner label.
- **Pending** (400, 11px monospace, 1.5, Tinta 60): `[PENDIENTE]` markers.

Weights: 400 everywhere; 500 only on the membership badge, the booking submit button, cookie buttons and `strong`; 450 on the booking panel title.

### Named Rules
**The Regular Voice Rule.** Hierarchy is size and tracking. Nothing is bold; 500 is reserved for small action or badge text.

**The Tight-Top Rule.** Tracking tightens as size grows (−0.015em titles, −0.02em headlines, −0.025/−0.03em display and figures). Body text is never tracked; only labels are tracked open (+0.16em).

**The Corner Label Rule.** The 11px uppercase label names a place, group, card or datum (card names, FAQ group titles, footer column heads, photo captions, address, schedule phase, step counter "01 — 04"). It sits at the corner of the thing it names, in Tinta 60 (Crema at 62–70% on dark bands).

## Layout

- **Container:** `min(100% − 2 × gutter, 100rem)`; gutter clamps 20→140px, which yields the reference's 140px side margins at 1440.
- **Section rhythm:** 72→120px block padding per section.
- **Section head:** title left, small datum (label or pending marker) right, baseline-aligned, 40→60px above content.
- **Grids:** 4-column for steps, question cards, memberships, portals and footer; 3-column for classes and ideas; asymmetric 1fr + fixed column (520–620px) for Recovery and founders bands. Gaps 16px (memberships), 20px (cards), 24px (questions), 28px (portals, map row).
- **Breakpoints:** 64rem (4 → 2 columns), 56.25rem (header collapses to logo + menu, mobile CTA bar appears, bands stack), 40rem (single column, CTAs go full width), 36rem/30rem (memberships and footer single column).
- **Mobile:** the hero photo fills the top and fades into Crema under the headline; a solid Crema bottom bar with a hairline carries the CTA and WhatsApp.

## Elevation & Depth

Light, not glass. Surfaces are solid and flat at rest; depth comes from radial halos behind content, hairlines between rows, and warm shadows on the few raised elements.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sombra** (`0 24px 60px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.1), 0 2px 6px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.04)`): booking panel, mobile menu, cookie card.
- **Sombra S** (`0 10px 28px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.05)`): floating WhatsApp button.
- **Card lift** (`0 18px 40px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.08–0.1)` with translateY −4px): hover on step and question cards.
- **Premium** (`0 24px 60px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.22)`): the highlighted membership card.
- **Button shadow** (`--sombra-boton`: `0 1px 2px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.1), 0 6px 14px -6px rgb(59 29 14 / 0.22)`): short, neutral lift on every button. No orange glow — it read as neon.

### No glass (client decision, 2026-09-30)

Translucency and blur read as generic; the site uses none. Every surface is solid: the header is a full-width Crema bar with a hairline underneath, the mobile bottom bar is a solid Crema bar with a hairline on top, the FAQ is a plain list between hairlines, overlays behind the menu and booking panel are a flat Tinta veil at 28 %, and the login card is solid Crema with a Naranja 100 border. The only blur left is the custom cursor's small halo.

### Named Rules

**The Warm Shadow Rule.** Every shadow is tinted Tinta or orange. No grey or black shadows, no hard offsets.

## Shapes

Circle and arch. Cards and panels use a 28px corner; inner panels (counter, notices, map button, pending markers) 14px; FAQ rows and widget boxes 16px; the cookie card 20px. Buttons, chips, badges, fields and the header are full pills (999px). Portal photos are arches: 180px top corners over 28px bottom corners (120px / 14px on mobile). Glyph details use 2px and 4px micro-radii inside circular 1.5px-stroke medallions. The closing section is a huge soft sun disc cut by the section edge, rising 60px on scroll.

**The Sun-Shape Rule.** New geometry comes from the circle, the arch and the pill. No sharp-cornered rectangles, no diagonal cuts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **CTA pill (signature):** Naranja 500 pill 64px tall, Tinta 19px label, with a 52px Naranja 900 arrow disc (Crema arrow) seated inside its right end, 6px from the edge (client decision, 2026-10-06: the old orange disc read as orange on orange). Short neutral shadow (`--sombra-boton`), never an orange glow. Hover: fill steps to Naranja 300 and the arrow nudges 3px right inside the disc. Compact variant 48px / 38px disc / 15px label for the header. On ≤40rem the normal pill is 56px / 44px disc, 16px, full width; in the mobile bar beside WhatsApp, 52px / 40px disc.
- **Ink button:** Tinta pill, Crema 500-weight text, 44px (cookie consent).
- **Text link:** Naranja 900 or Tinta with a 1px Naranja 300 underline offset 0.22em; underline turns Naranja 500 on hover.
- **Focus:** 3px Naranja 500 outline, 3–4px offset; Naranja 200/300 on dark bands.

### Chips
Crema pills on peach cards, 13px (8px 14px padding) or large 19px (12px 22px). Static, not interactive.

### Cards / Containers
- **Soft card:** Naranja 50, 28px corners, ~34px 30px padding, corner label then content. Hover lift on steps.
- **Question card:** Naranja 100, 28px, 300px min height, 26→34px question on top, answer and a 52px Naranja 500 arrow disc at the foot.
- **Membership card:** Naranja 50, 28px; Premium variant is Tinta with Crema text, Naranja 300 accents, a Naranja 500 badge overlapping the top edge, and taller (380px vs 352px).
- **Inner panel:** Naranja 50 or translucent cream, 14px corners.
- **Dashed card:** Crema with a 1px dashed Naranja 700 at 45% (the late schedule phase).

### Inputs / Fields
White pills with a Naranja 100 hairline, a 40px Naranja 50 icon disc at left, a 12px label above the 16px value. Focus: Naranja 300 hairline plus 3px Naranja 500 ring. Invalid: Naranja 900 hairline.

### Navigation
Full-width fixed header bar: solid Crema, 1px hairline underneath, content aligned to the page width (.envoltura), never shrinks on scroll; 15px Tinta links with a 1.5px Naranja 500 underline on hover and on the current page.

### Language switcher
In the header pill, between the links and the CTA: the current code (13px, +0.08em) with a small chevron in a pill that turns white 70% on hover/open. It opens a Crema card (14px corners, Naranja 100 hairline, Sombra) listing the four languages as code + native name; the current one sits on Naranja 100. From 68rem down the header links move into the menu (logo, language, CTA, menu button); in the menu sheet the languages are outline pills (Naranja 200), the current one filled Naranja 100.

### Glyphs (signature)
Outline medallions: a 1.5px Naranja 700 circle (52–56px) holding one geometric form (arc, ring, screen, line, dot, leaf, rectangle). Never pictorial icons.

### Portal photos (signature)
Arch-topped photos (180px top radius) in a 4-up gallery, each with an uppercase label caption below; the arch opens from the bottom as it scrolls in. Pending images are blurred and toned sepia into the palette, with a `[PENDIENTE]` marker at bottom-left.

### Map
Tostado panel, a Naranja 50 arch shape, 96px Naranja 200 grid lines, a Naranja 100 base strip, an orange halo and an 18px Naranja 500 pin that pulses once. Beside it, a Naranja 50 address card.

### FAQ
Title at left; at right, one tab per FAQ group (pills, active in Naranja 500) and the active group's questions as a plain list between hairlines; all closed by default.

### Bands
- **Recovery:** Naranja 50 field. Title at left, the two brief lines at right, then three rows (El reformer, La sauna, La sala de luz roja) alternating an arch visual with its explanation: panel-title heading, lead intro, em-dash bullets, and an "En tu membresía" 4-column strip (plan / amount, hairline dividers; 2×2 under 36rem). Each arch is a real photo or a 1.1px line drawing that traces on entering view: the reformer on a cream-to-peach ground, sauna benches over a rising orange heat, two LED panels over the Rojo glow.
- **Founders:** a slim Naranja 900 bar fixed above the header on every page while `fundadores.mostrar` is on (client decision, 2026-10-06): 20px Crema title, the places counter in Naranja 300, and an underlined "Ver ventajas" with a + that turns to ×. The benefits unfold downward over the header in four columns with 2px Naranja 300 rules (two under 64rem, one under 48rem). Its measured height (`--aviso-alto`) pushes the header and page tops down.
- **Footer:** Naranja 900, giant Crema wordmark, 4-column uppercase-labelled link columns, 13px base line.

### Motion (from the reference motion notes)
- **Load:** no loading screen; the page shows immediately (client decision, 2026-09-30: no generic "AI" effects).
- **Hero:** static. No masked line reveal, no blur-in, no parallax; the headline and photo are there from the first paint. Header never shrinks.
- **No page transitions, entrance animations, hover lifts, pulses or bobbing arrows** (client decision, 2026-09-30). Menus, dialogs and the cookie banner appear instantly. The only loop left is the dot next to "El estudio solo abre con alguien de SUNNA dentro", which the client asked for.
- **Cómo funciona:** on desktop the section pins and the four step cards travel horizontally with scroll; cards lean up to 4px toward the pointer. Mobile and reduced motion: plain stack.
- **Glyphs:** trace their line on entering view, 60ms stagger, no bounce.
- **Estudio:** each arch grows from the bottom as it enters; its contents drift 8%.
- **Booking panel:** stretches out of the button that opened it (320ms, corner rounding on the way) and shrinks back to it on close.
- **FAQ:** tabs per group; open row pushes the rest down (280ms); the + turns 45°.
- **Map:** the dot pulses once on entering view.
- **Closing sun:** rises 60px with scroll.
- **Pages:** crema cross-fade between pages (400ms, view transitions).
- **Not animated (client decisions):** prices do not count up.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes every displacement.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every visual value from the SUNNA Web reference canvas and every word from the brief.
- **Do** use the 11px uppercase +0.16em label to name the card, group, place or datum it sits on.
- **Do** mark missing content with the 11px monospace `[PENDIENTE]` marker instead of inventing it.
- **Do** keep all headlines at weight 400 with tightening tracking.
- **Do** seat the Naranja 900 arrow disc inside the CTA pill; it is the one action shape.
- **Do** use 28px corners on cards, 14/16px inside them, 999px on pills and 180px on arch photos.
- **Do** keep the film grain and warm-tinted shadows on every surface.
- **Do** respect reduced motion (halos stop breathing, scroll animations off).

### Don't:
- **Don't** set text in Naranja 500 or 700.
- **Don't** add dark surfaces beyond the Recovery band, the founders band, the footer and the Premium card.
- **Don't** use glass, translucency or backdrop blur on any surface.
- **Don't** use bold weights or pictorial icon sets; glyphs are geometric outlines.
- **Don't** use grey or black shadows or hard offset shadows.
- **Don't** introduce colors outside the orange ramp, cream, tostado and Tinta. The one exception is the Rojo ramp, and only in the red-light room.
